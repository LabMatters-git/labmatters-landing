The problem

Every graduation season, a lab loses years of know-how.

It walks out with the people. Students rotate through in months to a few years and projects renew every 6 to 12 months. What one person figured out rarely gets written down where the next person can find it.

Slow onboarding

New members spend months rebuilding procedures that already existed.

The same questions, every term

Senior researchers and technicians become the lab's search engine.

Avoidable incidents

Undocumented procedures lead to damaged equipment and safety risks.
